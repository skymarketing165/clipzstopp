Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

Summary

Clipzstopp Uploader only connects to accounts owned by Clipzstopp. It does not collect data from other people, has no user sign-ups, and does not sell or share any data.

What the tool accesses

  • TikTok: basic profile info of the Clipzstopp TikTok account (to confirm the right account is connected) and permission to upload and publish videos to that account.
  • Instagram: permission to publish Reels to the Clipzstopp Instagram account.
  • YouTube: permission to upload videos to the Clipzstopp YouTube channel.

How it is stored

Access tokens are stored only on the Clipzstopp team's own computer, in files readable only by that user account. They are never sent anywhere except back to the platform that issued them.

What we don't do

  • We don't collect, store or process data about viewers or other TikTok, Instagram or YouTube users.
  • We don't use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers on this website.
  • We don't sell, rent or share data with third parties.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time from the connected-apps settings of TikTok, Instagram or Google. Deleting the token files on the computer also removes it.

Contact

Send a direct message to @clipzstopp on Instagram.
